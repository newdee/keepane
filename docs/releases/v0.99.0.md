## Pictures in panes, and a theme in the terminal's own colours

### New
- **Pictures in panes.** A program in a pane can send pictures with kitty's graphics protocol, and keepane keeps them with the pane's text: a picture scrolls with it, goes into the history, is cut by a split, and comes back after `restart-server` or a reboot. A terminal that shows such pictures (UnifyTerm, kitty, WezTerm, ghostty) is sent each one when it first comes into view; any other (Windows Terminal, conhost) shows `▧ image 640×480` in its place. Attach from one of each and each draws its own.
- A picture is kept once on disk, beside the saved sessions (`images/<sha256>.<ending>`), once it has lasted two seconds (an animation's frames never reach the disk), and held in memory only while it is being sent. A picture sent again under the same number replaces the last in the terminal.
- New options: `kitty-graphics` (on by default; off drops the pictures as before) and `image-disk-limit` (1G: past it the pictures not shown longest go first).
- The dashboard draws a PNG itself in `[0]` and in a channel's files.
- A pane of another machine (`link-mirror`) shows its pictures too: they are asked of that machine when shown (up to 8 MiB a picture).
- A pane says `KEEPANE_GRAPHICS=kitty` while pictures are on, for programs that decide by the environment; a program that asks the terminal is answered.
- **`set -g theme terminal`**: every colour is the terminal's own (its text, background and 16 colours; the bar and borders in its colours by name), so a translucent window, a background picture or the terminal's theme shows through, and keepane changes with it. The other built-in themes paint the panes in colours of their own, which a terminal draws over its translucency. The default stays `paper-dark`.

### Changed
- On Windows the zip and both MSIs now hold `conpty.dll` and `OpenConsole.exe` beside `keepane.exe`: Microsoft's console host (MIT, licence in `ConPTY-LICENSE`), newer than Windows' own, which each pane now runs in. Windows' own drops what it does not know, kitty's pictures among it. Keep the three together; `keepane.exe` alone still runs, with Windows' own.
- As text a picture is blank: `capture-pane` (with `-e` too), copy mode, the phone page and an agent reading the screen see spaces where it is.
