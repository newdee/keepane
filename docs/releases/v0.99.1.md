## The picked line in the theme's colours; pictures in a frame; a share's preview

### Changed
- The picked line follows the theme. Every built-in theme now names `@pick` (its accent mixed a fifth into its paper) and sets `mode-style` to its ink on it, so keepane's lists and menus, the dashboard and the settings board, which drew the picked row in reverse video, all draw the theme's highlight; its text reads at 4.5:1 or more on it. `terminal` draws it black on the terminal's green. The default `mode-style` is paper-dark's (`fg=#cecabe,bg=#3f2624,bold`, was `fg=yellow,bg=black,bold`); a `mode-style` of your own still wins.
- A theme of your own that sets its paper or accent but no `@pick` gets one made of them.
- Pictures in the dashboard's previews (`[0]`, a channel's files, a share) take a frame the same size for every picture: as wide as the panel, up to 64 columns, 4:3. A picture is fitted inside and centred, the room around in the theme's `@bar`, so a wide picture and a tall one take the same room and what comes after stays where it was. Pictures drawn by UnifyTerm's `ut view` take the same frame.

### New
- Page 3 of the dashboard (what is shared): its `[4]` has two tabs, `[` / `]` to change. `Preview`, the first, shows the share's file (a picture, a text's lines, a folder's list) over how it stands (state, time left, times sent, who opened it) and its link; `Details` is everything of it with the code to scan, as before.
